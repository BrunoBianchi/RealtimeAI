      How Question Bank Data Is Collected

      Privacy-first, JEV-analyzed community intelligence.

      1. Post-Session JEV Analysis

      When an interview ends, RealTimeAI runs local JEV analysis over the audio transcript. It evaluates semantic relevance to identify legitimate, substantive interview questions and filters out small talk, greetings, and audio tests.

      2. Strict Anonymization

      Only the interviewer's questions are indexed. Your personal speech, answers, resume data, candidate names, contact details, and private URLs are stripped and never transmitted.

      3. Automatic by Default, Always in Your Control

      Community contribution is enabled by default so candidates worldwide benefit from real interview questions. You can toggle it off anytime below or in Settings.
